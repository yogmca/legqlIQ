# How to Get WhatsApp Business Approved Without Company Registration

## Overview

If your company is **not registered** (no business registration certificate, GST, or incorporation documents), you can still get WhatsApp Business Account approved by Meta. Here's how:

---

## Option 1: Use Personal/Sole Proprietor Approach (RECOMMENDED)

### What You Need:
- Valid phone number
- Personal identification (PAN card, Aadhaar)
- Proof of address
- Active website or social media presence
- Email address

### Steps:

#### 1. Set Up as Individual/Sole Proprietor

**Display Name:** `LegalIQ` (your brand name)

**Business Type:** Select "Sole Proprietorship" or "Individual"

**Business Category:** "Professional Services" or "Consulting Services"

#### 2. Complete Business Profile

Fill out these fields in MSG91/Facebook Business Manager:

```
Display Name: LegalIQ
Business Description: Legal consultation platform connecting clients with verified legal professionals
Category: Professional Services
Website: https://legaliq.in (or your domain)
Email: your-email@gmail.com (or professional email)
Phone: Your business phone number
Address: Your current address (can be home address for sole proprietor)
```

#### 3. Provide Alternative Documentation

Since you don't have company registration, provide:

**Option A - Website Proof:**
- Screenshot of your website homepage
- Domain registration details
- Website showing your business name and services

**Option B - Social Media Proof:**
- Facebook Business Page
- LinkedIn Company Page
- Instagram Business Account
- Show consistent branding with "LegalIQ"

**Option C - Identity Documents:**
- PAN Card (for India)
- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Driving License

**Option D - Utility Bills:**
- Electricity bill with your address
- Phone bill
- Bank statement showing business transactions

#### 4. Build Online Presence First

Before applying, ensure you have:

✅ **Active Website:** https://legaliq.in with clear business information

✅ **Facebook Business Page:**
- Create at https://www.facebook.com/pages/create
- Use name "LegalIQ"
- Add business description, contact info
- Post some content (3-5 posts minimum)

✅ **Google My Business:**
- Register at https://business.google.com
- Verify your business location
- Add photos and description

✅ **Professional Email:**
- Use domain email (support@legaliq.in) if possible
- Or professional Gmail (legaliq.services@gmail.com)

#### 5. Submit for Approval

1. Log in to MSG91 Dashboard
2. Go to WhatsApp Business Account setup
3. Fill business profile with above information
4. Select "Sole Proprietorship" as business type
5. Upload any available documents (website screenshots, PAN card, etc.)
6. Submit for review

## Option 2: Use Facebook Business Manager Verification

### Steps:

#### 1. Create Facebook Business Manager Account

1. Go to https://business.facebook.com
2. Click "Create Account"
3. Enter business name: "LegalIQ"
4. Enter your name and email
5. Complete setup

#### 2. Create Facebook Business Page

1. In Business Manager, go to "Pages"
2. Click "Add" → "Create New Page"
3. Page Name: "LegalIQ"
4. Category: "Professional Services"
5. Add description, contact info, website
6. Upload logo/cover photo
7. Publish page

#### 3. Verify Business with Facebook

Facebook offers multiple verification methods:

**Method A - Phone Verification:**
- Facebook will call your business phone
- Answer and enter verification code

**Method B - Email Verification:**
- Use domain email (e.g., support@legaliq.in)
- Facebook sends verification link

**Method C - Document Verification:**
- Upload utility bill with business address
- Upload PAN card or other ID
- Upload bank statement

#### 4. Connect WhatsApp Business

1. In Business Manager, go to "WhatsApp Accounts"
2. Click "Add" → "Create New WhatsApp Account"
3. Enter phone number
4. Verify with OTP
5. Complete business profile
6. Submit for review

## Option 3: Register as Startup/Unregistered Entity

### For India (Without Formal Registration):

#### 1. Get PAN Card in Your Name
- Apply at https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html
- Use your personal PAN if business PAN not available

#### 2. Open Business Bank Account
- Many banks offer current accounts for sole proprietors
- Required: PAN card, Aadhaar, address proof
- Account name can be "Your Name - LegalIQ"

#### 3. Get Udyam Registration (Optional but Helpful)
- Free MSME registration in India
- Apply at https://udyamregistration.gov.in
- Only requires Aadhaar and PAN
- Instant certificate
- **This gives you official business recognition!**

#### 4. Use These Documents for WhatsApp Approval
- Udyam registration certificate
- Bank account statement
- PAN card
- Website/social media proof

## Quick Udyam Registration Guide (FREE Official Business Certificate)

### What is Udyam?
- Government of India's MSME registration
- **Completely FREE**
- Instant certificate
- Gives official business recognition
- No company registration needed!

### How to Register:

1. **Visit:** https://udyamregistration.gov.in

2. **Click:** "For New Entrepreneurs who are not Registered yet as MSME"

3. **Enter:**
   - Aadhaar Number
   - Name (as per Aadhaar)
   - Mobile number

4. **Verify OTP**

5. **Fill Details:**
   - Business Name: LegalIQ
   - Type: Service
   - Category: Professional Services
   - PAN Number
   - Bank Account Details
   - Business Address

6. **Submit**

7. **Get Certificate:** Instant Udyam Registration Certificate with unique number

### Use This Certificate:
- Upload to MSG91/Meta as business proof
- Shows you're a recognized business entity
- Increases approval chances significantly
